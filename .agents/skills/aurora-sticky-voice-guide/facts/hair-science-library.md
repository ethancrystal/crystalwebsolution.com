# AURORA Hair Science Library

*The factual backbone. Every claim below is true and defensible. Weave these into beautiful, sticky prose — never quote them like a textbook.*

---

## 1. Sulphates — What They Actually Are

- **SLS (Sodium Lauryl Sulphate)** and **SLES (Sodium Laureth Sulphate)** are anionic surfactants — the same chemical family used in floor cleaners and industrial degreasers.
- They clean by binding to lipids and lifting them away. This includes the **sebum** your scalp produces to protect the hair shaft.
- The "squeaky clean" feeling after washing with sulphates = your scalp's protective oil layer has been stripped.
- Chronic sulphate use can cause sebaceous glands to overcompensate, producing **more** oil — the "I need to wash my hair every day" trap.

**Sticky angle:** *"Sulphates were engineered to clean engines. They're still doing that job — just on your scalp now."*

---

## 2. Plant-Derived Surfactants (What AURORA Uses Instead)

- **Coco-glucoside** — derived from coconut and fruit sugars. Non-stripping, biodegradable, suitable for sensitive scalps.
- **Decyl glucoside** — gentle, well-tolerated by colour-treated and curly hair.
- **Sodium cocoyl isethionate** — produces a soft, creamy lather without stripping. Common in premium cleansers.
- These produce **less foam** because foam comes from harsh foaming agents in sulphates — not from the cleansing action itself.

**Sticky angle:** *"Lather isn't cleaning. It's a feeling."*

---

## 3. Karachi Water — The Local Truth

- Karachi tap water is classified as **hard water** (high calcium and magnesium content), particularly in coastal and central districts.
- Mineral deposits bind to the hair shaft, causing **dullness, dryness, tangling, and resistance to conditioner.**
- Hard water reacts with sulphate surfactants to form **soap scum** that deposits on the scalp.
- Chelating agents (EDTA, sodium gluconate) and weekly clarifying treatments reduce mineral buildup.

**Sticky angle:** *"Your hair isn't worse than your mother's. The water is."*

---

## 4. The Hair Shaft — A Biology Reminder

- The visible hair shaft is **dead keratinized protein.** It cannot biologically "repair" itself.
- Products marketed as "repair" deliver **surface conditioning** — they fill cuticle gaps and smooth the surface.
- Hydrolysed proteins (wheat, rice, keratin) penetrate the cuticle and temporarily strengthen the shaft.
- Silicones (dimethicone) seal the cuticle and add slip — useful, but heavy silicones build up and require clarifying washes.

**Sticky angle:** *"Hair can't heal. But it can be returned to softness."*

---

## 5. The Scalp Is Living Skin

- The scalp has the highest density of sebaceous glands on the body.
- A healthy scalp microbiome contains balanced populations of **Malassezia** yeast and **Staphylococcus** bacteria — disruption causes dandruff, itch, and inflammation.
- Most "hair problems" are scalp problems: dryness, flakiness, sensitivity, and thinning all originate at the follicle.
- A 4-minute daily scalp massage has been linked to increased hair thickness in clinical studies.

**Sticky angle:** *"Healthy hair starts where you can't see it."*

---

## 6. Colour-Treated Hair

- Permanent colour lifts the cuticle and breaks disulphide bonds inside the cortex to deposit pigment.
- Sulphate shampoos accelerate colour fade significantly compared to sulphate-free alternatives.
- Cold-water rinses, sulphate-free cleansing, and weekly deep conditioning extend colour life by an average of **2–4 weeks.**
- UV exposure and chlorine are also major fade accelerators.

**Sticky angle:** *"Colour isn't the enemy. Aftercare is the missing chapter."*

---

## 7. Frizz — What It Really Is

- Frizz is caused by **moisture entering the hair shaft** through gaps in a raised cuticle.
- Dry hair exposed to humid air absorbs moisture rapidly, swelling and frizzing.
- Humectants (glycerin, panthenol) draw moisture in *before* humidity does.
- Sealing oils (argan, jojoba, marula) close the cuticle and lock hydration in.

**Sticky angle:** *"Frizz isn't a flaw. It's a message your scalp keeps trying to send."*

---

## 8. Argan Oil — Why It Earns Its Reputation

- Cold-pressed from the kernels of the Argania spinosa tree, native to Morocco.
- Composed of approximately **80% unsaturated fatty acids** — primarily oleic (omega-9) and linoleic (omega-6).
- Contains **vitamin E (tocopherols)** at higher levels than olive oil — an antioxidant against UV and pollution damage.
- Molecular size is small enough to penetrate the cuticle, unlike heavier oils that primarily coat.
- Genuine argan oil has a faint nutty aroma. Odourless = likely refined and stripped of benefits.

**Sticky angle:** *"Argan oil's job isn't to shine. It's to remember."*

---

## 9. The Two-to-Four Week Truth

- Switching from sulphate to sulphate-free triggers a scalp **adjustment period.**
- Initial weeks may include flatter hair, mild oiliness, or different texture — the scalp is rebalancing.
- Visible improvement typically arrives between **day 14 and day 28.**
- **Most customers churn at day 7 because no one told them this.** Communicate it relentlessly.

**Sticky angle:** *"You'll feel it on day three. You'll see it by Friday. You'll trust it by week three."*

---

## 10. Conditioner — How It Actually Works

- Conditioner contains **cationic (positively charged) molecules.**
- Damaged hair carries a **negative surface charge.**
- The conditioner binds to the most damaged sections — which is why conditioner appears to "target" damage.
- Apply from mid-length to ends, not to the scalp — the scalp produces its own conditioning sebum.

**Sticky angle:** *"Conditioner doesn't love your whole head. It loves the parts that need it most."*

---

## 11. Heat Damage

- Hair begins to suffer cuticle damage above **150°C (300°F).**
- Most straighteners and curlers run between 180–230°C.
- Heat protectants form a thermal barrier (silicones) and absorb some thermal energy.
- Repeated heat exposure permanently degrades keratin's helical structure — not reversible, only manageable.

**Sticky angle:** *"Heat doesn't ask permission. Heat protectant is how you give it boundaries."*

---

## 12. Hair Growth — The Honest Facts

- Hair grows approximately **1–1.5 cm per month** (12–18 cm per year).
- Growth happens at the **follicle**, not along the shaft.
- Real growth factors are internal: protein, iron, biotin, zinc, sleep, stress, scalp circulation, hormones.
- Scalp products support a healthy growth *environment* — they cannot accelerate biology.

**Sticky angle:** *"Hair grows from your scalp, not from a bottle. The bottle's job is to keep what grows."*

## Citation Discipline

- **Never invent statistics.** If you don't have a number, write the feeling instead.
- **Never claim "cures," "guarantees," or "permanent" results.**
- **Never name competitors directly.**
- **Always frame results in realistic time frames** — days, weeks, never overnight.

The fastest way to break a premium brand is to over-promise. Beauty + stickiness + truth is the only durable formula.
