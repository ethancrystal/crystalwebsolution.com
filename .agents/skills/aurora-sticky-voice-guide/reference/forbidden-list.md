# AURORA Forbidden Phrases (And Why)

*Every word on this list has been disqualified for a specific reason. Don't override unless the user explicitly approves.*

---

## Hype Clichés
| Word/Phrase | Why It's Banned |
|-------------|-----------------|
| "Game-changer" | Used by every shampoo brand from 2014 onward. Dead. |
| "Holy grail" | Religious overtones, plus completely overused. |
| "Must-have" | Salesy. Pushy. Cheap. |
| "Magical" / "Miracle" | Sounds fake. Violates the Trust pillar. |
| "Revolutionary" | Premium brands don't shout. They demonstrate. |
| "Breakthrough" | Sounds like a pharmaceutical ad. |
| "The best" / "#1 in Pakistan" | Cannot be substantiated. Legal risk. |

## Fear-Mongering
| Word/Phrase | Why It's Banned |
|-------------|-----------------|
| "Toxic" | Scientifically loose. Reads as panic-marketing. |
| "Chemicals" | Everything is a chemical, including water. Reads as unscientific. |
| "Poison" / "Dangerous" | Premium brands educate; they don't terrify. |
| "Detox your scalp" | Detox is a meaningless marketing term in cosmetic chemistry. |
| "Ruins your hair" | Too absolute. Tell the mechanism instead. |

## Cheap Intimacy
| Word/Phrase | Why It's Banned |
|-------------|-----------------|
| "Hey babe" / "Hi hun" | Off-brand. Cheap intimacy. |
| "Ladies" / "Girlies" | Patronising. Use "you" instead. |
| "Queen" | Borrowed from 2018 Instagram, doesn't fit AURORA. |
| "Slay" | Trendy, dated, and tonally wrong for premium beauty. |
| "Obsessed" | Overused influencer-speak. |
| "Iconic" / "Vibes only" | Filler words with no meaning. |

## Empty Promises
| Word/Phrase | Why It's Banned |
|-------------|-----------------|
| "Cures dandruff" | Medical claim. Illegal in most jurisdictions for cosmetic products. |
| "Guarantees hair growth" | Cannot be substantiated. Hair growth is biological. |
| "Permanent results" | Hair is biological. Nothing is permanent. |
| "Overnight transformation" | Violates the Two-Week Truth. |
| "100% sulphate-free" | Reduce to "sulphate-free." Numbers without context look hollow. |

## Generic Openers (Caption Killers)
- "Hey beauties!"
- "Hi loves!"
- "Raise your hand if..."
- "Who else?"
- "Tag a friend who..."
- "Comment below if..."

These hooks signal *every other brand on Instagram.* AURORA opens with a feeling, a question that names an unspoken truth, or a sensory observation.

---

## What to Use Instead

| Tired Phrase | AURORA Replacement |
|--------------|--------------------|
| "Game-changer" | "The bottle that changed my Sundays." |
| "Holy grail" | "The one I keep buying." |
| "Toxic ingredients" | "Ingredients that don't belong on a scalp." |
| "Hey babes!" | *(open with a truth instead)* |
| "100% sulphate-free!" | "Cleansed with plant-derived surfactants." |
| "Miracle results!" | "Two weeks. Then you'll know." |
| "Best shampoo in Pakistan!" | "Built for Karachi water, Karachi weather, and the hair that lives through both." |
