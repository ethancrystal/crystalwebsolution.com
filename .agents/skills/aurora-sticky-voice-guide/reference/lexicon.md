## The Banned List (Never Use)

**Hype clichés:** game-changer · holy grail · must-have · magical · miracle · bestseller · revolutionary · breakthrough · life-changing · mind-blowing · the best ever

**Fear language:** detox · toxic · chemicals · poison · dangerous · harmful · ruin · destroy

**Cheap intimacy:** ladies · babe · hun · girlies · queen · slay · obsessed · iconic · vibes only

**False promises:** cure · permanent · guaranteed · 100% · forever · instant · overnight

**Generic openers:** "Hey beauties!" · "Hi loves!" · "Who else?" · "Raise your hand if..."

## Why the Banned List Matters
Every banned word is a linguistic dead zone where a thousand other shampoo brands already live. AURORA's voice survives by refusing to compete in the dead zones.
