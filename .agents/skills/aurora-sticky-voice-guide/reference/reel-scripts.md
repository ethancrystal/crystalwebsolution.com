**VO:** *"Sulphates were originally engineered as industrial degreasers."*

**Beat 2 (4s):** Cut to AURORA bottle. Gentle pour into palm. Soft creamy lather.
**VO:** *"AURORA cleanses with plant-derived surfactants. Less foam. More care."*

**Beat 3 (4s):** Slow motion of soft, finished hair catching light.
**VO:** *"Your scalp has been waiting for someone to listen to it."*

**Closer (2s):** Text overlay: *"Awaken your radiance."*
**CTA in caption:** DM **SOFT** for your routine.

## Reel Production Rules

- **Always subtitle.** Most viewers watch on mute.
- **Real voice. Real hands. Real Karachi light.** Never AI voiceover for emotional Reels.
- **No glitch effects, no aggressive zooms, no fast cuts.** Movement should feel like silk.
- **One Reel = one idea = one feeling.**
- **End every Reel with a DM keyword** in the caption — never a hard sell.
