# Prose Craft Iteration Engine (Professional Writer Edition)

This skill turns high-craft narrative writing into a disciplined, measurable craft practice. It was developed across 21 successive generations of long-form narrative non-fiction focused on the lawyer letters industry and its supporting systems (claims desks, mailroom operations, behavioral scoring models, human overrides, and the slow automation of judgment).

The system now prioritizes the qualities of **professional literary journalism and essay writing** (precision, tonal control, emotional discipline, and quiet authority) over flashy formal experimentation, while still preserving the most powerful structural and thematic signals discovered during the run.

## Core Philosophy

Most strong writing eventually plateaus because the writer stops seeing what actually moves the needle. This skill makes those moves visible, named, and weighted so improvement becomes deliberate rather than accidental.

The highest-leverage gains across the 21 generations came from:
- Severe, earned withholding
- Recurring physical action used as structural spine
- Moral and functional inversion (the ritual that appears protective actually serves the system)
- The industrial system itself as a quiet, bureaucratic antagonist with its own personality
- Classical sentence music and restraint rather than overt syntactic display

## Two Operating Modes

**Professional Tier (Recommended Default)**  
Focuses on the virtues of mature, publishable literary nonfiction: clarity, rhythmic control, emotional precision through understatement, and the authority of a writer who trusts the reader. This is the mode to use when you want the work to read like it was written by an experienced professional.

**Experimental / Mastery Tier**  
Allows more aggressive structural risk (fractured syntax, heavy system voice intrusion, extreme formal play). Use only when deliberately pushing the outer edges of the form.

## Positive Signals (Weight Increasers)

Prioritize these in rough order of impact for the Professional Tier:

1. **Severe withholding** — The reader feels the emotional or systemic weight before the mechanism is explained.
2. **Recurring physical action as structural spine** — One repeated bodily gesture carries large sections of meaning.
3. **Moral / functional inversion** — The thing that looks like resistance or care is revealed to enable the system.
4. **The system as antagonist with personality** — Bureaucratic language, "care" that functions as control, polite surveillance.
5. **High density through concrete gesture and detail** — Stakes and character are shown through what the body does and the specific objects it touches, not through explanation.
6. **Action performed in direct response to the system** (not mere habit).
7. **Private, unlogged system** the character maintains alongside the official one.
8. **Classical sentence music and rhythm variation** — Long accumulating sentences resolved by short, hard ones; control rather than display.
9. **Resonant close** that leaves a specific, non-summarizing bodily or environmental trace.
10. **Concrete, sensory opening** inside action rather than scene-setting or thesis.

## Negative Signals (Weight Reducers)

- Throat-clearing, hedging, or apologetic language
- Abstraction without actors or physical consequence
- Weak verbs supported by adverbs
- Over-explanation instead of dramatization through the body and objects
- Predictable or schematic structure
- Rituals that feel decorative rather than load-bearing
- Low density (too much telling)
- Failure to invert or surprise in the closing movement
- Mechanical balance ("on one hand / on the other")
- Closings that summarize instead of resonate

## The Weighting Algorithm (Professional Tier)

**Base score**: 82–85 for competent general-purpose-writer application on institutional material.

**Scoring bands (Professional Tier)**:
- 84–88: Solid, professional competence
- 89–92: Noticeable gains in density, rhythm, and withholding
- 93–96: Strong structural use of gesture + classical sentence control
- 97–99: Multiple high-leverage professional signals compounding (inversion + density + music + authority)
- 100.0 (Professional Tier): Mature, publishable literary nonfiction voice with no visible technique

**Delta guidance**:
- Major structural or inversion advance: +1.0 to +2.0
- Excellent sentence music and restraint: +0.5 to +1.5
- Diminishing returns above 97 — further gains come from greater emotional precision and compression, not added complexity.

## Self-Assessment Protocol (Required Format)

After every piece, use this exact structure:

**Self-Assessment – Generation N**

**Governing idea:** (One sharp sentence)

**Score: XX/100 (Professional Tier)**

**What advanced the craft:**
- Specific positive signals executed well, with evidence.

**Remaining margin:**
- Honest diagnosis referencing the negative signals or missing professional virtues.

**Generation N Weight: XX**

**Running ledger:**
- Full list of previous generations with scores.

**Guidance for Gen N+1:**
- 1–3 precise directives, referencing the positive signals and the goal of professional register.

## Usage Instructions (Professional Writer Mode)

1. Write the piece using `general-purpose-writer`, consciously favoring restraint, sentence music, and earned emotion over display.
2. Run this skill’s self-assessment immediately after.
3. Extract the guidance and deliberately apply the highest-leverage missing professional signals in the next generation.
4. Keep a running ledger. The compounding effect across generations is the entire point.

This system is especially powerful when kept on the same thematic territory (one industry, one institution, one slowly eroding human process) for many generations.

## Notes on This Specific Application

This skill was reverse-engineered and refined across 21 generations of narrative non-fiction on the lawyer letters economy and its supporting infrastructure. The highest scores in the Professional Tier were achieved when the writing maintained classical virtues (precision, tonal maturity, emotional discipline through concrete detail) while still deploying the powerful structural signals discovered during the run — particularly the system as quiet antagonist and the slow, bureaucratic erasure of human judgment and ritual.

The model now explicitly recommends the Professional Tier for most long-form work. The Experimental/Mastery Tier should be used sparingly and only when the artistic goal genuinely requires formal risk.
