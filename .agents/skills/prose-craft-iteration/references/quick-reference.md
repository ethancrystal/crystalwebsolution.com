# Prose Craft Iteration — Quick Reference (Professional Tier)

## When to Use This Skill
Use after every draft when you want the writing to feel like it was produced by an experienced, mature professional writer (literary journalism / long-form essay level).

**Default mode**: Professional Tier (recommended for most work).

## Core Philosophy (One Line)
Make the industrial system the antagonist and show the slow erasure of human presence through concrete physical actions and small administrative details.

## Top Positive Signals (Professional Tier)
1. Severe withholding + classical restraint
2. Recurring physical action as structural spine
3. Moral/functional inversion
4. System as antagonist with personality (polite coercion, "care" as control)
5. High density through gesture and specific objects
6. Strong sentence music and rhythm (long → short resolution)
7. Resonant close with a specific bodily or environmental trace
8. Concrete, sensory opening inside action

## Things That Lose Points in Professional Mode
- Overt syntactic fracturing or showy technique
- Over-explanation instead of showing through the body
- Throat-clearing or hedging language
- Mechanical balance ("on one hand...")
- Summarizing endings
- Low density / telling instead of gesture

## Quick Scoring Bands (Professional Tier)
- 84–88: Solid professional competence
- 89–92: Good density + rhythm gains
- 93–96: Strong structure + sentence control
- 97–99: Multiple high-leverage signals compounding
- 100.0: Mature, publishable literary nonfiction voice

## Recommended Practice
- Stay on the same thematic territory for many generations.
- After every piece, run the self-assessment immediately.
- Apply the highest-leverage missing professional signals in the next draft.
- Keep a running ledger.

## One-Sentence Reminder
The best work in this mode feels precise, humane, quietly devastating, and written by someone who has lived with the material for a long time.
