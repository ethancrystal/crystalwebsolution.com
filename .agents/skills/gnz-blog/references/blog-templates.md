# Blog templates for GNZ Enterprises

Six formats. Each has a worked skeleton. Pick the format in Phase 3 of the protocol; apply the skeleton; fill with GNZ voice from `brand.md` and craft from the inherited `award-columnist` protocol.

Templates are skeletons. The prose inside still has to clear Phase 6 standards — specific verbs, concrete examples, plain English, no banned phrases.

---

## Template 1 — SEO pillar article (2,500–4,000 words)

**Use when:** the target keyword is category-level — *"what is SEO,"* *"complete guide to local SEO,"* *"conversion rate optimization for e-commerce."* The post needs to comprehensively cover a topic to compete for high-volume keywords.

```
# [Title]: [Subtitle with year / modifier]

> **Meta Title:** [55–60 chars, primary keyword first, "| GNZ" optional]
> **Meta Description:** [150–160 chars, value + soft CTA]
> **Target Keywords:** [primary], [secondary 1], [secondary 2], [secondary 3]
> **URL Slug:** /blog/[primary-keyword-hyphenated]

---

[OPENING — 3 to 5 short paragraphs]
- Paragraph 1: three or four specific scenarios in short sentences
- Paragraph 2: name the question / pain point
- Paragraph 3: state the answer in one sentence
- Paragraph 4: a credibility paragraph with a real stat from a recognized source
- Paragraph 5: one-line preview of what the guide will cover

---

## What Is [X]?
[Definition in plain English. A metaphor or comparison the reader already knows. The three or four functions it serves, in prose. Define by what it is NOT.]

## Why [X] Matters for [Audience]
[The stakes. Specific business outcomes. Numbers from real GNZ engagements or recognized industry studies.]

## How [X] Works
[The mechanism. A framework or process model. 3-5 stages, each with one short paragraph and one specific example.]

## Common [X] Mistakes — and How to Avoid Them
[6-8 bold-led mistakes. Each: name the mistake, explain why it happens, give the fix in one sentence.]

**[Mistake 1].** [Why it happens]. [The fix in one sentence].

---

[INLINE CTA #1 after value delivery]
> Want help applying this to your business? [Book a free strategy call →](https://gnzenterprises.vercel.app/contact)

---

## How to Get Started with [X]
[The action sequence. Steps 1–5 or 1–7, ordered. Each step a bold lead-in + 2-3 sentences. Concrete tools named where relevant.]

## How Much Does [X] Cost?
[Cost ranges by approach: DIY, freelancer, agency. Specific dollar ranges. Honest about tradeoffs. Position GNZ as the integrated option that pays for itself.]

---

[INLINE CTA #2 after cost section]
> Curious what an integrated program would cost for your business? [Get a custom proposal →](https://gnzenterprises.vercel.app/contact)

---

## [X] vs [Related Concept]
[Disambiguation. Two paragraphs comparing. End with "sometimes the answer is both."]

## Tools and Resources for [X]
[3-7 named tools. For each: one sentence on what it does, one on when to choose it.]

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is [X] in simple terms?
[2-4 sentences]

### How long does [X] take to show results?
[2-4 sentences with specific timeframes]

### Can I do [X] myself?
[2-4 sentences honestly addressing DIY vs. pro]

### How is GNZ different from other [X] providers?
[2-4 sentences positioning GNZ's integrated approach]

### What's the ROI of [X]?
[2-4 sentences with ranges from real engagements]

### How much does [X] cost with GNZ?
[2-4 sentences with starting points]

---

## Ready to Get Serious About [X]?

[3-4 sentence closer. Recap the value. Name the next step. The third inline CTA.]

[Book a free strategy call →](https://gnzenterprises.vercel.app/contact)
```

## Template 2 — How-to guide (1,200–2,000 words)

**Use when:** the target keyword is action-oriented — *"how to set up Google Ads conversion tracking,"* *"how to do keyword research for local SEO,"* *"how to write a marketing email that converts."* The post is teaching one specific skill.

```
# How to [Do the Thing]: A Step-by-Step Guide for [Audience]

> **Meta Title:** How to [Do the Thing]: Step-by-Step Guide (2026)
> **Meta Description:** [150–160 chars]
> **Target Keywords:** [primary], [secondary 1], [secondary 2]
> **URL Slug:** /blog/how-to-[do-the-thing]

---

[OPENING — 2 to 3 short paragraphs]
- Paragraph 1: the situation the reader is in (specific). Why they're searching this
- Paragraph 2: what they'll be able to do by the end. The promise
- Paragraph 3: prerequisites. What you need before starting (account, tool, access)

## What You'll Need
- [Tool / account 1]
- [Tool / account 2]
- [Time estimate]
- [Cost estimate if any]

## Step 1: [Action]
[2-3 short paragraphs. The action stated plainly. Why it matters. Common confusion addressed. A specific example.]

## Step 2: [Action]
[Same structure]

## Step 3: [Action]
[Same structure]

[Continue for 5-9 steps total. Each step is one specific action, not a category of actions.]

---

[INLINE CTA #1 after the first half of steps — placed at the moment the reader has gotten value but realizes there's complexity]
> Want us to handle this for you instead? [Book a free strategy call →](https://gnzenterprises.vercel.app/contact)

---

## Step N: [Final action]
[Often verification / measurement step. How do you know it worked?]

## Common Pitfalls
[3-5 bold-led pitfalls specific to this how-to. Each: what goes wrong, why, the fix.]

**[Pitfall 1].** [Sentence on what it looks like]. [Sentence on the fix].

## When to Hire a Specialist
[2-3 paragraphs honestly addressing when DIY is fine and when professional help saves money in the long run. This is the bottom-funnel transition.]

---

## Get [Service] Done Right

[3-4 sentence closer. Position GNZ for readers who'd rather have it handled.]

[Book a free strategy call →](https://gnzenterprises.vercel.app/contact)

---

> **Schema:** HowTo + Article
> **Internal links:** [related how-to posts, related services page]
> **External authority links:** [2-3 to tool documentation, official platform guides]
> **Image suggestions:**
> - Hero: [process completion screenshot/illustration]
> - Step screenshots: [list which steps need screenshots]
> - Final result: [outcome visual]
```

## Template 3 — Case study (800–1,500 words)

**Use when:** showcasing a specific client win. Bottom-funnel — readers near purchase decision want proof.

```
# How [Client Type] Achieved [Specific Outcome] in [Timeframe]

> **Meta Title:** Case Study: [Outcome] for [Industry] | GNZ
> **Meta Description:** [150–160 chars highlighting the result]
> **Target Keywords:** [industry] marketing case study, [service] results, [outcome] case study
> **URL Slug:** /case-studies/[client-type]-[outcome]

---

[OPENING — 2 paragraphs]
- Paragraph 1: the headline result in one sentence with specific numbers
- Paragraph 2: name the client (or anonymized version), the industry, the size, and the situation they were in

## The Challenge

[2-3 paragraphs setting up the problem. What was broken. What they had tried. Why it wasn't working. Specific metrics that were unacceptable.]

## The Approach

[3-5 paragraphs walking through what GNZ did. Each paragraph is one move:
- Discovery / audit findings
- Strategy decision (with the reasoning)
- Tactical execution
- Iteration based on early data
- Scaling

Bold-led sub-points where appropriate. Named tools, named tactics, named decisions.]

## The Results

[Bold-led list of outcomes with specific numbers:]

**[Metric 1]:** [Before → After] in [Timeframe]
**[Metric 2]:** [Before → After]
**[Metric 3]:** [Before → After]

[1-2 paragraphs unpacking which result mattered most and why.]

## What Made It Work

[3-5 bold-led insights — the non-obvious things that drove the results. This is where the case study earns its educational value beyond "GNZ did good." The reader takes lessons they can apply, even if they don't hire GNZ.]

**[Insight 1].** [Why it mattered].
**[Insight 2].** [Why it mattered].
**[Insight 3].** [Why it mattered].

---

## Could This Work for Your Business?

[2-3 sentence closer. Honest about which businesses are good fits for this approach. CTA.]

[Book a free strategy call →](https://gnzenterprises.vercel.app/contact)

---

> **Schema:** Article (with `creativeWorkType: "CaseStudy"`)
> **Internal links:** [services pages relevant to this case study, other case studies in adjacent industries]
> **External authority links:** [1-2 to industry benchmarks the results compare favorably against]
> **Image suggestions:**
> - Hero: [results visualization / dashboard screenshot anonymized]
> - Before/after chart
> - Workflow or process diagram
>
> *Results vary based on industry, market conditions, and execution. This case study is illustrative of approach, not a guarantee of outcomes.*
```

## Template 4 — Industry op-ed (700–1,000 words)

**Use when:** taking a position on an industry topic — a Google update, a platform change, an industry trend, a contrarian take. Pure thought leadership. Earns links and authority over time.

```
# [Sharp Headline That Contains the Position]

> **Meta Title:** [Sharp position, ≤60 chars]
> **Meta Description:** [150–160 chars]
> **Target Keywords:** [topic keyword], [related discussion keywords]
> **URL Slug:** /blog/[topic-hyphenated]

---

[NO H2 SECTIONS — prose throughout, like an op-ed. 6-10 paragraphs.]

[Paragraph 1: a specific scene, news event, or numerical fact that sets up the position]

[Paragraph 2: name the conventional wisdom on this topic]

[Paragraph 3: state where GNZ thinks the conventional wisdom is wrong, or incomplete, or only true under conditions people aren't naming]

[Paragraphs 4-7: the argument. Each paragraph one move. Evidence, examples, named patterns. Take the reader along the path]

[Paragraph 8: smart-reader pushback addressed]

[Paragraph 9: the larger implication — what should marketers / business owners actually do]

[Final paragraph: the kicker. One of the four canon kicker shapes (loop-back / reframe / implication / under-modified verdict). Quotable, not staged.]

---

[Single CTA at the end — not embedded mid-piece, because op-eds are voice-led and a mid-piece CTA breaks the spell]

> If you'd rather have a partner who's actually paying attention to this stuff — [we should talk →](https://gnzenterprises.vercel.app/contact)

---

> **Schema:** Article + OpinionNewsArticle
> **Internal links:** [1-2 related op-eds or services pages]
> **External authority links:** [3-5 to the news event, the studies cited, the platform announcements]
> **Image suggestions:**
> - Hero: [editorial-style image, not a generic stock photo]
>
> *Opinions expressed are GNZ's own and reflect our current thinking. Market conditions change; positions evolve.*
```

## Template 5 — Listicle / roundup (1,200–2,500 words)

**Use when:** the target keyword is comparison or "best of" — *"best email marketing tools for small businesses,"* *"7 SEO mistakes that kill your ranking,"* *"top 12 marketing dashboards in 2026."*

```
# [N] [Tools / Tactics / Mistakes / Trends] for [Audience] in [Year]

> **Meta Title:** [N] [Item Type] for [Audience] (2026)
> **Meta Description:** [150–160 chars]
> **Target Keywords:** [primary listicle keyword], [individual item keywords]
> **URL Slug:** /blog/[number]-[item-type]-[audience]

---

[OPENING — 2 paragraphs]
- Paragraph 1: why this list matters. Specific stat or situation
- Paragraph 2: what makes this list different (real testing, ranked vs. random, criteria used). Honesty about the methodology buys credibility

## How We Ranked These
[2-3 sentences naming the criteria. E.g., "We ranked these based on (1) total feature depth, (2) value for small budgets, (3) ease of setup for non-technical users."]

## 1. [Top item] — Best Overall
[2-4 short paragraphs. What it is. Who it's for. What makes it #1. One specific concrete example or use case. Pros and cons in plain prose, not bullets (bold-led "Strengths:" / "Limitations:" works).]

## 2. [Second item] — Best for [Use Case]
[Same structure]

[Continue through the list. Each item earns its place with reasoning, not assertion.]

---

[INLINE CTA #1 after item 5 or halfway through — placed at the natural mid-list pause]
> Need help picking and implementing the right tools for your business? [Book a free strategy call →](https://gnzenterprises.vercel.app/contact)

---

[Continue with remaining items]

## Honorable Mentions
[2-4 tools that almost made the list, with one sentence each on why they didn't]

## How to Pick the Right [Tool/Tactic] for Your Business
[3-5 short paragraphs. The decision framework. Not "consider your needs" — actual criteria with thresholds. "Under $5M revenue? Start with [X]. B2B with long sales cycles? [Y] is the better fit."]

---

## Get an Expert Pick for Your Situation

[Closer. Position GNZ as the partner who picks and implements.]

[Book a free strategy call →](https://gnzenterprises.vercel.app/contact)

---

> **Schema:** Article + ItemList
> **Internal links:** [individual reviews of the tools listed, related listicles]
> **External authority links:** [the tool websites themselves, third-party reviews, G2/Capterra]
> **Image suggestions:**
> - Hero: comparison-grid visual
> - Per item: tool logo / screenshot
> - Decision framework: flowchart visual
```

## Template 6 — Founder / agency essay (1,200–3,500 words)

**Use when:** the post is voice-led, narrative, brand-building. Not for SEO — for trust and authority. Often comes from the founder or agency leadership.

```
# [Personal / Provocative Headline]

> **Meta Title:** [the headline, possibly tightened]
> **Meta Description:** [150–160 chars, voice-forward]
> **Target Keywords:** [only if there's a natural fit — sometimes founder essays don't target keywords]
> **URL Slug:** /blog/[topic-hyphenated]

---

[OPENING — 2 to 4 paragraphs]
- A specific scene or moment from agency work, founder experience, client engagement
- The personal anchor the rest of the piece builds from
- Voice established immediately — first person, candid, observational

[3 to 6 H2 sections, each a beat in the argument]

## [Section 1]
[Each section has its own internal arc — small opening, development, mini-kicker. The transitions between sections do real connective work, not just "Moreover."]

## [Section 2]
[Continued]

[Maybe one section that names what other agencies / common wisdom gets wrong, with specifics]

[Maybe one section that walks through a specific client situation as illustration]

[Maybe one section that names what GNZ believes and why, position-taken]

[KICKER paragraph — often loops back to the opening scene, or opens onto a question the next essay will answer. Quotable. Not summary.]

---

[Single CTA at the end, conversational tone]

> If you want to work with people who think about marketing this way — [we'd love to hear from you](https://gnzenterprises.vercel.app/contact).

---

> **Schema:** Article + BlogPosting (with author markup tied to the founder)
> **Internal links:** [1-3 related essays or about-page]
> **External authority links:** [as relevant to the references made]
> **Image suggestions:**
> - Hero: editorial photo, not stock
> - Author photo embedded
```

## Selection guide

| If the request is... | Use template |
|----------------------|--------------|
| "Complete guide to X" / "Everything you need to know about X" | 1 — SEO pillar |
| "How to do X" / "Step-by-step X" | 2 — How-to guide |
| "How [client] achieved [result]" / "Case study on X" | 3 — Case study |
| "Why X is wrong" / "The truth about X" / "Take on [news]" | 4 — Industry op-ed |
| "Best X for Y" / "Top N X" / "X vs Y vs Z" | 5 — Listicle |
| "[Founder name] on X" / "Why we do X this way" / "What we've learned about X" | 6 — Founder essay |

When the request fits two templates, default to the format that will rank better in Google for the target keyword — unless the user has explicitly asked for the voice-led format.

When the request fits none, default to Template 1 (pillar) and adapt — pillars are the most versatile structure for a digital marketing agency blog.
