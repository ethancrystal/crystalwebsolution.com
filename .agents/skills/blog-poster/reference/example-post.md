## Short Answer

A California small-business owner can use a flat-fee attorney demand letter to recover an unpaid invoice in 7–14 days for under $200 — without filing suit, without retainer fees, and without a $400+ hourly consultation. Most disputes settle within two weeks of the letter arriving.

## What is a demand letter and why does it work?

A demand letter is a formal written request, sent on attorney letterhead, demanding payment or performance by a specific date. The credibility comes from the attorney's name on the page: the recipient knows their next correspondence might come from a courthouse, not a collections agent.

## How long does it take to get results?

Typical response window: 7–14 days from delivery. Under California Civil Code § 1788 (Rosenthal Act), the recipient has a reasonable period to respond before further collection action.

## What does a Talk-to-My-Lawyer letter cost?

Flat fee, $200 per letter. No retainer. Letter is drafted, reviewed by a licensed California attorney, and sent via certified mail with tracking.

## What if they ignore the letter?

The next step is small claims court (under $12,500) or filing in superior court. The demand letter itself becomes evidence of pre-litigation good faith — which courts and juries view favorably.
