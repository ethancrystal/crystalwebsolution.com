# User Canon

This is the personal reference library for the `columnist` skill. Drop in articles, essays, blog posts, newsletter pieces, or any prose you love and want the skill to learn from.

## How to populate

Save articles as `.md` or `.txt` files in this folder. One article per file. Suggested naming:

```
graham-do-things-that-dont-scale.md
housel-best-story-wins.md
brooks-eulogy-virtues.md
mystery-newsletter-i-can-never-stop-thinking-about.md
```

A short header at the top of each file helps the skill use it well:

```markdown
# Title of the piece
Author: [Writer]
Source: [URL or publication]
Why I love it: [one or two sentences — what about it lands for you]
Voice notes: [optional — "the lede earns the click," "the kicker is the line I steal in conversation," etc.]

---

[Full text of the article, pasted in.]
```

## How the skill uses this folder

In **Phase 1 (Inventory)**, the skill scans this folder for any pieces that overlap with the current topic, voice, or argument shape.

In **Phase 3 (Pick the voice)**, the skill considers whether any piece here is a closer reference than the named writers in the voice library — because your taste is more useful than my taxonomy.

The skill borrows **structural moves** (how the lede works, how transitions earn their place, how the kicker resolves) — never long verbatim phrases, since these are pieces you've gathered for your own study.

## What to put in here

Don't try to assemble a complete canon up front. Add pieces as you encounter them. The folder grows over time, like a writer's commonplace book. Even three or four pieces in here is enough to materially sharpen the skill's voice.

Worth including:
- Pieces whose **opening sentence you remember years later**
- Pieces whose **structure you've thought about** even more than their content
- Pieces you've reread on purpose
- Pieces that made you change a draft of your own

Less worth including:
- Pieces you only liked because you agreed with them (agreement isn't craft)
- Pieces that are good information but not good prose
- Pieces whose voice you don't actually want to write in

## A note on copyright

Articles gathered here are for your personal study and the personal use of this skill. Don't redistribute the folder publicly. The skill is instructed to learn structural moves rather than reproduce verbatim text, which is the right craft-study practice regardless of legal context.
