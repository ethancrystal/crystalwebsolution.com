## LinkedIn

Most "how much does AI automation cost?" answers are either a chatbot SaaS price or a vague "it depends."

In 2026, US studio work usually lands in planning bands like ~$5k–$15k (simple workflow), ~$15k–$50k (production agent with evals + guardrails), and $50k+ (multi-system). What moves the quote isn't the model — it's integrations, data readiness, evals, and whether you need a retainer after the build.

We broke down the bands, DIY vs in-house vs studio, and how to brief a build without wasting a discovery cycle:
https://www.cdsportswearinc.com/blog/how-much-does-ai-automation-cost

Got a high-volume workflow and a budget band? Send a short brief — we'll say if it's not a fit.

If you're comparing DIY tools to a studio build, there's no honest single sticker price. For US SMB/mid-market work in 2026, quotes usually sit in ~$5k–$50k+ bands depending on simple workflow vs production agent vs multi-system.

What moves the number more than model choice: integrations, data readiness, evals/guardrails, and post-launch support.
