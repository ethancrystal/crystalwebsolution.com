### 3.2 Three defects found — filed as CRY-27 and CRY-28

**CRY-27 (Urgent) — `crystalwebsolution.com` DNS is broken and there is no 301.**
<https://linear.app/crystal-web-solution/issue/CRY-27>

The domain is **still registered** — RDAP (rdap.verisign.com, queried 2026-09-01): registered
2024-02-07, expires **2027-02-07**, last changed 2026-07-27. It has not lapsed.

What is broken is the delegation. The registry lists four nameservers **split across two
providers**: `JUNE.NS.CLOUDFLARE.COM`, `URIAH.NS.CLOUDFLARE.COM`, `NS1.VERCEL-DNS.COM`,
`NS2.VERCEL-DNS.COM`. All four return SERVFAIL when queried directly over UDP/53. Google and
Cloudflare DoH both return `Status: 2` (SERVFAIL, *not* NXDOMAIN) with
`EDE(22): No Reachable Authority at delegation crystalwebsolution.com`. Control: `ns1.vercel-dns.com`
answers NOERROR for `cdsportswearusa.com`, so the nameservers are healthy — there is simply no
zone for the old domain on any of them.

> The repair steps in CRY-27 are withdrawn.
