## 10. What each proposal must include

Please answer in the same order so proposals are easier to compare:

1. Your understanding of the problem and desired outcomes.
2. Recommended approach, including alternatives and trade-offs.
3. Relevant project examples: your role, the client’s problem, and verifiable results where permission allows.
4. Proposed team, responsibilities, and who will do the work.
5. Deliverables, assumptions, exclusions, and client responsibilities.
6. Project phases, milestones, dependencies, and review time.
7. Itemized pricing, payment terms, optional items, recurring costs, and change-request process.
8. Testing, accessibility, security, migration, and launch plan.
9. Post-launch warranty, support, response times, and ownership / handover.
10. Risks you see in the brief and questions that would change your recommendation.

## 11. Evaluation scorecard

Set the weights **before** reviewing proposals. Adjust them to fit the project; the example below totals 100%.

| Criterion | Weight | Proposal A (1–5) | A weighted points | Proposal B (1–5) | B weighted points | Notes / evidence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

| Relevant, verifiable experience | 20% | [ ] | [score × 4] | [ ] | [score × 4] | [ ] |
| Approach, team, and delivery plan | 20% | [ ] | [score × 4] | [ ] | [score × 4] | [ ] |
| Price, value, and clarity of assumptions | 15% | [ ] | [score × 3] | [ ] | [score × 3] | [ ] |
| Timeline and review dependencies | 10% | [ ] | [score × 2] | [ ] | [score × 2] | [ ] |
| Support, documentation, and ownership | 10% | [ ] | [score × 2] | [ ] | [score × 2] | [ ] |
| **Total (maximum 500 points)** | **100%** |  | **[ ]** |  | **[ ]** |  |

**Scoring:** Use 1 = does not address the requirement, 3 = adequate and supported, 5 = strong and evidenced. Weighted points = score × weight expressed as a whole-number multiplier (for example, score 4 on a 25% criterion = 100 points). Record evidence, not just impressions. A high score does not override a failed mandatory requirement.
