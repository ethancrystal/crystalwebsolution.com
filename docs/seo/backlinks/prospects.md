# Outreach prospects — cdsportswearusa.com

**Empty on purpose.** The 2026-09-01 and 2026-09-02 runs did not research
prospects: the old domain's profile turned out to be a PBN blast (see
`pbn-watch.md`), and prospecting against a domain that is six days old with no
published content to link to would produce a list nobody can act on.

The Backlinks lane restarts from zero against `cdsportswearusa.com` once the
first linkable asset exists — the RFP guide (CRY-22) is the natural first one.

## Format for entries

Each prospect needs, before it can enter outreach:

- **Domain** and the **specific page** the link would live on
- **Target URL on our site** (one, from the registry)
- **Reason to link** — why *their* reader benefits
- **Asset** — what we are offering (guide, data, tool, case study)
- **Score** — relevance / geography / authority / organic visibility /
  editorial legitimacy / relationship strength, minus spam signals, paid-link
  language, excessive outbound links
- **Source** of every number, with date

Categories in priority order: local/regional business ecosystems · technology
and implementation partners · existing client and project relationships ·
editorial and educational resources · linkable assets.

Outreach is **sent by MJ, or with MJ's explicit yes for each message.** The run
drafts; it never sends.
